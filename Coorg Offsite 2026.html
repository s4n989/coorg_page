Ground rules

A few things to keep in mind across the three days.

Code of Conduct POSH policy

  1. Read the policies again

    Give the Code of Conduct and POSH policies a quick read before you go.

  2. Drink responsibly

    Enjoy yourselves, but any behaviour while intoxicated is your own responsibility, so let’s keep it respectful.

  3. Roommates are a surprise

    Roommates are assigned across teams, and there’s a fun game attached to the reveal. Come in with an open mind.

  4. Room service is on you

    It isn’t covered by the company. If you order, the person the room is assigned to pays the bill.

  5. Stay on the property

    Please stay within the property for the whole stay. It’s for everyone’s safety, and everything good is happening right there.
